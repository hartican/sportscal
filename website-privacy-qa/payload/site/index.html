A FEW PRACTICAL DETAILS

GOOD TO KNOW.

Is this a website or an app?

Both. This website introduces Nothing Sport and shows a selection of current fixtures. The full web app lives at nothingsport.app. You can use it in a browser or add it to your home screen.

Will I keep my existing account and follows?

Open the same app at nothingsport.app and use your existing sign-in. The website does not create a separate account or follow list. Install the .app page, as shown in the guide.

Can I hide scores?

Yes. Scores on this website start hidden; use the Scores toggle above the fixtures. The full app has its own score visibility controls.

Where does the match information come from?

The website reads Nothing Sport’s existing match feed. Match details include source links and the last available check times. If an update cannot be confirmed, the website shows that explicitly.

Do I watch the sport inside Nothing Sport?

Nothing Sport helps you find and follow the action. Viewing links take you to the listed broadcaster or streaming service. Their availability, subscriptions and viewing terms apply.
