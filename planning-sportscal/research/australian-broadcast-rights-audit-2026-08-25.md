# Australian broadcast and streaming rights audit — 25 August 2026

## Scope and publication rule

This audit covers all 19 exposed top-level sports in `data/follow-directory/manifest.v1.json` and the concrete competitions currently represented in `data/events.json` and `data/major-events.v1.json`. Sources were checked on **25–26 August 2026 (Australia/Sydney)**. Field Hockey, Gymnastics and the misleading Other Games Sports fallback are no longer user-facing; their historical Commonwealth Games records remain internally compatible.

Only first-party evidence is used: the broadcaster or streaming service, competition owner, league, federation or national governing body. A broad provider sports page establishes a competition only when it names that competition. It does not justify assigning that provider to every event in the sport.

The button policy should be:

1. Use fixture-specific verified rights when present.
2. Otherwise use a current Australian competition-level mapping.
3. Show the first complete paid/subscription option as the primary `Watch on [logo]` button, then retain free-to-air/free-streaming and other paid alternatives in ordered metadata.
4. Do not use a foreign-territory service as an Australian fallback.
5. When neither fixture nor competition rights are verified, show `Viewing TBC` and require an event-level refresh.
6. Completed fixtures use `Replay on [provider]` only when the cited service confirms on-demand or replay access.

This means “all sports have a provider” cannot safely be implemented as one provider per sport. Rugby Union is a valid broad Stan Sport mapping; motorsport, football, cricket, golf, boxing and several smaller sports require competition-level resolution.

## Current-data corrections and blockers

These are audit findings, not code changes.

- The Rugby Union card `South Africa v All Blacks` currently says `Broadcast details TBC`; it is in the New Zealand Tour of South Africa inventory on Stan and should resolve to **Stan Sport**.
- `WRC Safari Rally 2027 — Eurosport` is not an Australian mapping. Stan is the current Australian WRC service.
- `Paris-Dakar Rally 2026 — FOX Sports / Fox` conflicts with SBS’s current Australian Dakar/World Rally-Raid slate. Use SBS for Australian territory after matching the exact event record.
- `Goodwood Festival of Speed — Sky Sports / WatchESPN` is a foreign-market assignment. The official Goodwood stream and YouTube coverage are the verified Australian-accessible path.
- `UCI Downhill MTB World Cup — SuperSport` is an African-territory label, not Australian evidence. The UCI has previously listed Stan for Australian World Championships but the 2026 World Series round still needs a current event-level source before publication.
- `Telemark World Cup Finals — Eurosport` is not established for Australia. FIS publishes event-specific YouTube/FIS TV streams; resolve the exact event or show `Viewing TBC`.
- Generic `FIS broadcast` is not a launchable provider. Convert only after the specific event page confirms FIS TV/YouTube availability in Australia.
- The Surfing key `wsl` means **World Surf League**, not English football’s Women’s Super League. Stan’s `WSL` catalogue entry is football and must never be applied to surfing. The app’s present `WSL / Paramount+` surfing label has no primary-source support in this audit.
- `X Games Skateboarding — ESPN`, `Pipe Masters — ESPN`, and the two big-wave cards need fresh event-specific Australian evidence; a generic ESPN string is insufficient to generate a Kayo button.
- Cricket cards for away tours must not inherit Cricket Australia’s domestic rights package. Keep Kayo/Foxtel only when the exact series is verified.
- NBA League Pass, DAZN, Disney+, BINGE, 10 streaming, Prime Video, FIS TV, FIAWEC+ and official/YouTube streams are absent from the current `VIEWING_PROVIDERS` registry and require provider records before the corresponding buttons can be rendered correctly.

## Recommended validation contract

- Every future watchable record must resolve by `territory + competitionId + season/date`, not `sportId` alone.
- Reject `SuperSport`, `Eurosport`, `Sky Sports`, `WatchESPN`, `WatchNRL`, `Watch AFL` and other foreign/ROW services when the current user territory is `AU`, unless the source explicitly includes Australia.
- Reject ambiguous initialisms such as `WSL` unless the competition identity distinguishes World Surf League from Women’s Super League football.
- A provider marked `completeCoverage: true` may become the primary paid action. A selection provider can only become primary when the exact fixture is on its schedule.
- Preserve a territory exception on the record rather than burying it in display copy.
- Store `sourceUrl`, `verifiedAt`, `liveOrReplay`, `accessType`, `territory`, `coverageScope` and `coverageCompleteness` with every resolved option.
- Expire competition rights at their stated end date and fail to `Viewing TBC` rather than carrying stale rights into a new season.
- Test date-bound transitions, especially Netball’s Foxtel/Kayo/BINGE 2026 rights changing to Nine/9Now/Stan on 1 January 2027.
