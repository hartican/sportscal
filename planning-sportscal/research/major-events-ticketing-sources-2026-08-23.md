# Major events and ticketing source audit - 23 August 2026

## Publication policy

- Event dates come from the event organiser, governing body, or official schedule.
- Ticket CTAs use a direct verified seller endpoint. Event and governing-body pages remain provenance links only.
- A missing seller endpoint produces no CTA. A date not confirmed by a current primary source remains TBC.
- Sources were checked on 23 August 2026 (Australia/Sydney).

## Exclusions

- Generic NRL, AFL, Formula One, tennis, and venue homepages are not ticket CTAs.
- Search pages and vendor homepages are not accepted as fixture-level ticket endpoints.
- The previously bundled 7 March 2027 Australian Grand Prix watch date remains unpublished because the current official 2027 ticket page does not confirm it.
