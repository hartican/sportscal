## Editorial operating notes

- The L0 hook should expose the sharpest **verified** stake, correction or uncertainty. A high Nothingscore cannot turn a provisional matchup into a fact.
- Result, draw, table, seed, injury and selection facts need explicit expiry checkpoints. Historical and format facts may persist, but should move behind fresher developments once a live stage begins.
- Unsupported catalogue records need an editorial state such as `research_blocked` or `record_needs_correction`; generic sport facts are not a substitute for evidence that the event exists.
- Reuse one persistent thread across related cards—Argentina's two Tests, the three NRL finals weeks, Baku qualifying/race, the Bledisloe pair—then attach event-specific facts and expiry rules. This produces continuity without repeating the same paragraph.
- Suggested fact/source/thread IDs are semantic and date-scoped where facts can change. Store source publication/access dates and preserve source-to-fact links; do not store copied article prose as reusable card copy.
