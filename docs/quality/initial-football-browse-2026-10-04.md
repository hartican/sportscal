## Native saved-profile reload defect

The added real reload check found a second, materially larger defect. A club follow persisted correctly in the separate native `entityFollows` section after the view-only save, then disappeared on reload. The initial test mistakenly called the mutating `loadPreferences()` as a read; replacing that call with direct storage inspection did not remove the actual reload failure. The retained direct-read red trace proves the loss on the normal boot path. Cricket/Follow migration constructs an empty graph before the old loader considers native graph sections. The repair hydrates native graph sections before existing migrations; an explicitly supplied graph, including an empty or replacement one, retains precedence. No old follows are unioned back in. It preserves local follow/mute/unfollow, disabled competition, viewing and learning sections and keeps exclusion, Results and Remind OFF. It does not recover earlier lost data or prove authenticated cross-device/server persistence.
