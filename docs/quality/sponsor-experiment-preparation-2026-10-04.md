## One-page decision brief

Recommendation: retain **one four-week, fixed-fee contextual sponsorship** with a relevant local Australian sports retailer reached through an existing relationship. Prepare one creative and one destination; agree the fee only after actual audience and fulfilment costs are known. Use existing off-app invoicing. Do not build billing, an advertiser dashboard, an auction or another analytics integration.

The future placement is one dismissible support tile below existing Feed content, outside the organic fixture list. Its visible heading is **Advertisement · Paid sponsorship**. Copy identifies the business and its actual offer, with a plain Visit link. It does not claim NS endorsement of performance, accuracy, betting predictions or a guaranteed audience. The sponsor provides approved artwork or a text-only creative. A sponsor pays for the stated placement and dates; fixture facts, rankings, editorial, Follow admission, watch destinations and spoiler settings keep their existing owners.

Commercial hosting eligibility and permission to use NS's sports data/artwork remain unresolved launch dependencies. Official hosting/disclosure findings are in the [source assessment and time limits](sponsor-source-assessment-2026-10-04.md). If the current hosting entitlement cannot support commercial use without a subscription, **hold launch under the owner's no-subscription instruction**. This is not permission to upgrade. A clearly labelled advertisement does not itself clear sports data or marks.

Before making a paid offer, produce one dated cohort-only baseline from real invited participants, excluding owner/QA accounts. Show useful-return numerator, denominator, dates, the measured population and missing data. Do not extrapolate the September four-account observation, equate discovery exposures with reach, or claim a percentage represents all visitors. The current report does not verify invited membership or Australian residence. No fresh customer aggregate was queried for this preparation.

Success is a worthwhile positive net contribution, an actual paying sponsor requesting renewal, no substantiated trust/disclosure complaint, and at most 30 minutes of weekly owner administration. Record the fee and direct incremental cash costs in settled A$, with owner hours separately; agree a minimum acceptable contribution before signing. One renewal request is evidence about that sponsor, not product-market fit. Stop immediately for a substantiated misleading claim or broken destination; stop at four weeks if there is no worthwhile renewal, fulfilment becomes bespoke, or administration exceeds the cap. Do not auto-renew.

One consolidated launch approval can cover the prospect, claims/creative, fee, dates, permitted placement scope and stop conditions once prerequisites are evidenced. No decision is required now. During the test, one paragraph in the existing weekly exception readout replaces separate sponsor dashboards or meetings.

## Current code evidence and measurement limit

Raw product-event SELECT is not granted to ordinary authenticated users. These are source and isolated-test observations, not a fresh production permissions audit.

**Verified in source:** the current client starts measurement automatically for signed-in participating accounts and lets them opt out. Signed-out and opted-out use remains functional without client telemetry. This is not an explicit opt-in-only contract; do not silently change the approved participation default for a sponsor. **Verified limitation:** the [current SQL](../../supabase/nothingsports-pilot-readout.sql) reads measured accounts in a rolling 28-day window; it has no reviewed invited-cohort allowlist or owner/QA exclusion. `curator`, `hybrid` and `completist` are declared survey segments, not an invitation register. The [readout model](../../config/pilot-readout.js) explicitly warns that owner activity may be included. Existing useful return means a fixture check or watch decision on at least two distinct Sydney dates. It is not D7 retention, playback, Australian residency or incremental sponsor sales.

**Verified limitation:** no `sponsor_action` category is accepted by the current JavaScript or database event contracts. Reusing `opportunity_exposed`, `watch_decision` or ordinary `feed_action` for an advertisement would contaminate the core decision-loop denominator. The technical work below is therefore a future implementation brief, not a claim that sponsor measurement already exists.
