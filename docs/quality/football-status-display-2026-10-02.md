A future timestamp no longer counts as fresh. Confirmed final results remain final without another poll; cancelled/postponed/suspended/abandoned fixtures cannot acquire live state. Normal future kickoffs and Starts Soon remain intact. This changes neither Follow admission nor underlying facts/results. The live-only view no longer treats unconfirmed EPL schedule time as live evidence. The current official EPL schedule adapter publishes upcoming/completed states; this module does not add a live-score source or infer an undocumented provider flag. Where no fresh explicit live observation exists, awaiting-update is the intended honest state.
