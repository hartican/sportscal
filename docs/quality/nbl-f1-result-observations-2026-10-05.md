## Why this fix belongs in the current architecture

The app already separates canonical sports adapters, known fixture writes, publication and browser rendering. The demonstrated gap was disagreement between those boundaries about what counts as a new result observation. This delivery reuses the existing known-fixture fact/date rule at NBL publication and keeps F1 schedule evidence separate from result evidence. It adds no new browser API, provider, scheduler, polling route, automatic retry policy, database schema, credential or paid service. Archived real NFL/NHL live observations are test inputs only, never ingested as current games.

The NBL date regression initially passed at the scoped writer and then failed at the actual general publisher; that evidence is retained. Older cache checks also assumed particular NFL/NHL games would still be live. Their actual source transitions exposed those assumptions. The corrected rehearsal tests current finished cards and separately retains genuine historical live/privacy/staleness controls. The first NHL observer wrongly required the complete source sentence even though the existing finished card shows a numeric result under named participants; the corrected observer checks source-ordered scores and the real completed lifecycle. The first hosted F1 check also required the full source title, while the existing qualifying card displays the shortened 'Bahrain GP · Qualifying' title. The retained observation shows the correct Sepang venue and original result evidence. The final local/hosted checks verify the actual session and relocated venue independently, plus the full supplied podium and Results privacy. No product renderer, timeout, freshness threshold, retry, required failure, or release gate was relaxed.
