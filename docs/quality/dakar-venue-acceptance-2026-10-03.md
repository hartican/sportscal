Official evidence: ASO 2026 FIM programme (`https://storage-aso.lequipe.fr/ASO/motorSports_ccr/sr-fim-dakar26.pdf`) and 2027 itinerary (`https://www.dakar.com/en/overall-route?iframe=true`).
