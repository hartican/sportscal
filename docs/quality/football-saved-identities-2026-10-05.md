## Investigation and rework

The investigation began at 06:39 UTC with a 15-minute cap, extended to 30 minutes at 06:55. The correct failing caller and passing repair were obtained within that extended diagnosis window; broader verification and release ran afterwards. Initial controlled attempts did not establish their test account and never reached a fixture request, so they are not evidence of app failure. The prior account test tried to replace a frozen method; the corrected seam now exercises actual client storage and session changes with isolated local APIs.

One wider run stopped at a ten-second reload-startup wait. The unchanged timeout and added failure-only diagnostics were retained; the complete diagnostic rerun passes all 42 cases without a timeout waiver or shipped retry. This isolated interruption has no captured cause and is not claimed resolved. The first extra offline check lacked a worker-cached identity resource because it loaded before worker control. Explicitly warming that same existing resource makes the cache-specific check valid; never-loaded offline data remains a stated limit. No debug instrumentation ships in the app.

The canonical local Follow route recomputed two unrelated dated audit/horizon windows; their initially clean HEAD bytes were restored and the differences retained in `unrelated-derived-window-retention.json`. No live source observation, fixture or source date was refreshed. Total model time, billable tokens and end-to-end owner effort are not separately instrumented, so no saving percentage is claimed.
