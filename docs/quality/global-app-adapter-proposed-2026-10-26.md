The wrapper combines outer cancellation with the SDK's original signal and **chains the original timing callback**, preserving the grouped store's actual text-byte receipts. It requires complete ordered successful HTTP/body/JSON phases and <=16MiB combined actual text bytes. Parsed grouped group/edge counts and full decoder validation retain the existing proposed5,000groups/10,000edges/16per-key limits. Legacy replies cannot masquerade as the grouped qualification.
