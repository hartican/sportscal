# Card style notes

These notes belong to `styles/follow-feed-rework.css`. They are kept here to preserve the rationale without adding it to the app download.

- Preserve the original official square asset while giving its wordmark room.
- Sports selection collapses independently of the always-available sections.
- Share the matchup hierarchy across the minimal Feed and Events paths.
- The minimised state mounts only its row; ordinary card composition is skipped.
- Rating guidance lives in About; empty feedback never reserves card space.

Moving these notes changes no CSS rule, selector, declaration or styling behaviour.
