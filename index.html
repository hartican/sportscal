Nothingsport — Smart sports feed

The sports-fanatic mate who knows what you’re into.

nothingsport curates live sport around your tastes, learning from what you follow and rate so the feed gets more useful over time. It surfaces the matches and events most worth your attention, keeps results hidden until you choose to reveal them, and helps you catch major live moments without trawling every fixture. Open Tune to shape the mix or temporarily hide sports, and open any sport for a focused view. Built around AEST/AEDT by default, it adapts to other time zones.
