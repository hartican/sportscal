Nothingsport — Smart sports feed

Follow live sport without the clutter.

nothingsport is a smarter way to follow live sport without wading through low-value fixtures. Its personalised feed surfaces the matches and events worth your attention, keeps browsing spoiler-safe, and helps you catch major live moments without the clutter. Choose Froth when you want every eligible fixture, or focus on one sport for its full fixture view; the pinned filter can stay visible as you scroll or tuck away while your selection remains active. Built around AEST/AEDT by default, it adapts to other time zones.
