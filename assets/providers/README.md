# Viewing-provider marks

These marks are bundled only to identify the destination of a user-initiated
`Watch on …` link. If a local mark cannot load, the provider name replaces it
inside the same reserved frame without moving the control.

- `kayo-sports-negative.svg` — Kayo Sports official media-site footer asset,
  retrieved from
  `https://media.kayosports.com.au/content/experience-fragments/kayo/site/footer/master/_jcr_content/root/footer_177152923/logo.coreimg.svg/1705295428407/kayosports-logo-negative.svg`
  on 25 August 2026.
- `stan-sport.jpg` — resized from Stan's official `Stan_StanSport_RGB.jpg`
  support attachment at
  `https://help.stan.com.au/hc/article_attachments/13072485483663`, retrieved on
  25 August 2026.
- `foxtel.svg` and `paramount-plus.svg` — scalable brand glyphs retrieved from
  the Simple Icons public SVG catalogue on 25 August 2026. Their destination
  links remain the providers' own Australian sites.

The marks remain the property of their respective owners and are not modified
other than the Stan source image being resized for mobile delivery.
