# Men’s golf major artwork

The asset manifest records each source, author, rights, modifications and output hash. Only Aronimink’s 2026 PGA Championship layout is verified: twenty fairway portions represent eighteen numbered holes, with the separate practice range excluded. Other editions use the licensed golf glyph. No geometry is borrowed across editions, including 2027 Fields Ranch East and the altered Old Course.

The original Masters file is a raster wrapper; its complete PNG is retained as a PNG. The PGA image and native Open SVG retain the complete undated marks. The U.S. Open uses a neutral golf identity because a usable authorised brand image was unavailable. These marks remain editorial references with their owners’ rights and trademarks retained.

Every venue SVG contains genuine paths and transparent backgrounds. Flaticon glyph attribution appears in the footer and About. New tee times, entries and round results are not inferred from the calendar.
