# WRC venue artwork

The manifest records individual authors, sources, rights and modifications. Sardegna highlights one prominent stage: Lerno–Sa Conchedda–Monti di Alà (SS8/11, 24.14 km), including the iconic Lerno jump. This is not a full-rally overview. Geometry comes from the organiser’s individual 2026 stage map, with its start circle and finish flag verifying direction. Its 2,048 segments are simplified to 152 with a maximum 0.55 PDF-unit deviation, then rotated 18 degrees and uniformly scaled to fit the horizontal panel. Start and finish icons remain at the verified endpoints. Road transfers, satellite imagery, labels and logos are removed. The exterior and enclosed areas remain transparent, with no embedded raster. Never reuse it for another edition or configuration.

The source map is an official reference, not an open-licensed stock asset. Original media rights remain with Rally Italia Sardegna. Only factual stage geometry is presented for editorial identification, with the source credited in the footer and About. The neutral helmet reuses the existing Apache-2.0 Sporticon motorsport glyph; licence notice remains in `assets/licenses/`.

Other editions use the helmet fallback until their actual route geometry is verified. Monte-Carlo 2027 currently has a published itinerary but no verified overview geometry. The source itinerary labels its stage times indicative; the card retains date-only precision and discloses the provisional timetable. No route is inferred from stage names. Flaticon searches did not yield an accessible, verified rally-specific free asset for this delivery; existing Flaticon credits remain unchanged.
