Circuit de la Sarthe
===================

The full 13.626 km Le Mans circuit, including the Mulsanne chicanes, is distinct from MotoGP’s Bugatti configuration. Native white SVG paths follow only the official centreline. No map background or enclosed fill remains. The full existing ACO mark and licensed Sporticon helmet are reused. Individual sources, retained rights, modifications and hashes are in asset-manifest.json.
