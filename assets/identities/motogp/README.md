# MotoGP venue pilot artwork

All assets are SVG paths with transparent outer backgrounds. The 15 supplied PNGs were vector traced with vtracer 0.6.15 (spline, alpha threshold 128 for silhouettes, path precision 2); no raster image is embedded. The badge retains its complete colours and lettering. Silhouettes are white, with a black motorcycle identity glyph and a white panel derivative.

Licence: [Flaticon attribution licence](https://www.flaticon.com/media/license/license.pdf). Free PNG sources permit adaptations with author and Flaticon credits. Visible credits are in the footer and About; individual links and retained supplied category references are in `asset-manifest.json`.

Venue assignment uses official track UUIDs, never a country or fuzzy venue name. Reviewed against MotoGP official track maps on 2026-10-02. Austria, Catalunya, new Argentina and all other unmatched configurations use the motorcycle fallback. Indianapolis and Termas artwork are retained but unused. Bugatti is distinct from Circuit de la Sarthe.
