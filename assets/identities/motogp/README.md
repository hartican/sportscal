# MotoGP venue artwork

Track-only white SVG linework with transparent enclosed areas and outer backgrounds, cropped viewBoxes and no embedded raster. The full-colour badge and monochrome motorcycle remain unchanged. The supplied `philip-island.png` is the current Phillip Island source. Other compatible PNGs are from [berkahicon’s outline pack](https://www.flaticon.com/packs/circuit-13796880), found through the supplied MotoGP search. Generic or incorrect icon titles never establish circuit identity.

PNG linework is alpha-traced with vtracer 0.6.15 (threshold 128, exact connected contours, precision 2). Official MotoGP simple maps supply missing geometry. Austria retains the separately licensed 2022-onwards MotoGP path, including the Münzer chicane; its outdated simple map and inactive straight are excluded. All SVGs are checked for exterior and interior transparency in Chromium and WebKit.

Assignments use verified configuration UUIDs. A narrow reviewed Brazil override requires the exact circuit, configuration, season, length and source URL: the current official event page and classifications confirm the 3835m map despite the API inactive flag. All 22 current-season circuits have artwork. The 2027 Buenos Aires replacement remains unavailable: the published historical outline cannot establish the rebuilt configuration, and neither Termas nor the future F1 layout is substituted.

Visible author, source, licence and modification credits are in About; Flaticon credits also remain in the footer. Original supplied category references and previous source provenance are retained in `asset-manifest.json`. Adaptations of Flaticon PNGs use its attribution licence. Official map references retain original media rights; they are not described as Flaticon licensed. `spielberg-motogp.svg` and its modifications are CC BY-SA 3.0, with all credited authors listed.
