# WSL identity assets

The white wave is a genuine three-path SVG traced from the licensed Flaticon icon by manshagraphics. Visible credits appear in the footer and About; individual source, licence and modifications are recorded in asset-manifest.json. No PNG is embedded. The complete official WSL wordmark is retained as a local transparent PNG for editorial identification on a dark frame.

No break or coastline geometry has been verified. Venue panels use the wave fallback, never an invented coastline or another break. Country colours reuse the reviewed F1 palettes; unknown palettes are neutral.
