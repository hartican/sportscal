NOTHING SPORT — INDIVIDUAL PNG EXPORTS

LOGOS
Primary and hero-skier logos use the original, flat, transparent source masters. Includes original 2000 × 2000 canvases, tightly cropped native artwork, and 1600 / 1024 / 512 / 256 px widths.

ICONS
Eight colourways: dark full colour, light full colour, clear glass, greyscale dark, greyscale light, inverted, high-contrast neon, monochrome white.
App icons (n + SPORT): 1024, 512, 256, 192, 180, 152, 144, 128, 108, 96, 76, 72, 64 px square.
Favicons (n only): 64, 48, 32, 16 px square, plus a 1024 px master.

TRANSPARENCY
Rounded tile exteriors are transparent. Glass interiors use partial alpha (about 24–46% opacity away from the rim), while the artwork remains opaque. Rim highlights are more opaque. The layers folder includes the glass background and the flat artwork separately.
These static PNGs let underlying content show through. Live background blur/refraction must be provided by the host app or website.

SOURCE & REVIEW
The individual glass icons were reconstructed from the approved design sheet at higher resolution, then exported with separate foreground/background opacity. Small rendering differences from the sheet are possible. Lettering and n symbols are flat.
preview-light-dark.png shows the same actual PNG files over light and dark backgrounds.
transparency-checks.json verifies opaque artwork and semi-transparent tile pixels. manifest.json lists every PNG, dimension and checksum.
Built-in image-generation tool prompts are in generation-prompts.txt.
